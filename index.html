Frequently Asked Questions

What are your operating hours and location? +

We are open daily from Tuesday to Sunday, 2:00 PM to 10:00 PM. We are situated at our cozy al fresco stall with open-air seating for all our visitors.

Do you offer non-dairy or oat milk options? +

Yes! We have oat milk and soy alternatives available for both our coffee and matcha selections upon request at the bar.

Can we book the patio for small gatherings or group study? +

Absolutely. You can reserve tables for study groups, barkada hangouts, or intimate celebrations using our contact form below or through direct message.
